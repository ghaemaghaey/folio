Folio

Calm reading, open source.

A distraction-free reader for PDF and EPUB. Paper-like themes, real typography, chrome that gets out of the way.

Why

Reading software should feel like a book,
not a browser tab.

Most desktop readers fall into two traps: feature-heavy but ugly, or pretty on mobile only. Folio started as an answer to that gap — a small, serious app you can trust with long sessions.

Good reading software should feel like a book, not a browser tab full of toolbars.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

PDF & EPUB

Read both formats in one app. PDF rendered via WebAssembly, EPUB with full continuous scroll and chapter navigation.

Bookshelf

Your library at a glance. Cover thumbnails, last-read position, and automatic file remapping when you move things around.

Three Themes

Sepia for evening, light for day, dark for night. Carefully tuned paper tones — not just inverted colors.

Persian & English

Full RTL support with Vazirmatn, Samim, Shabnam, and more. Plus carefully chosen English reading faces like Literata.

Distraction-Free

Chrome hides when you read. Header and footer appear on edge hover or press M. The page is the experience.

Reading Guide

An optional screen-fixed leading line to keep your place. Adjustable height, hue, and lock position.
